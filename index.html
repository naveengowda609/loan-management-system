About This App

✏️ Edit Loan Details
Edit borrower name, father's name, address, loan amount, monthly interest rate, notes, and status information.
📅 Date Range Tracking
Track loans between specific date ranges. Monthly payments are automatically generated based on your start and end dates.
💰 Track Monthly Payments
View monthly payments with actual dates. Check boxes when monthly interest is received. Auto-calculates total received amount.
📊 View Loan Summary
See complete loan overview with loan amount, total expected, total received, and pending amounts in one place.
🔐 Secure Login
Your personal account with login and registration. Each user has their own loan records and data.
💾 Auto Save
All data is automatically saved locally on your browser. No internet required after first load.
